PROJECT 01 / KITCHEN REMODEL

Kitchen transformation, built in phases

First, we refinished the existing cabinets and installed a new backsplash. When the homeowners were ready for the next chapter, we returned to complete a larger cabinetry transformation with a dark, modern finish.

✦ The backsplash belongs to the earlier phase; the cabinetry transformation came later.
PROJECT 02 / CUSTOM CABINET DOORS

A fresh direction for an existing kitchen

An existing kitchen gained a completely different character through updated cabinetry, fresh shaker-style lines, and carefully selected hardware.

✦ A thoughtful update can change the feel of the entire room.
PROJECT 03 / BATHROOM & SHOWER

A bathroom rebuilt with purpose

We replaced the deteriorated shower backing and the shower system, then refreshed the surrounding finishes. The progress photograph documents the exposed damage; the finished bathroom shows the completed transformation.

✦ From the underlying work to the visible finish, the details matter.
PROJECT 04 / CUSTOM PANTRY & CABINETRY

From an ordinary doorway to custom storage

We removed the existing doorway opening and created a full-height built-in wood pantry, complete with shelves, interior pull-out trays, and deep drawers. The green shaker kitchen doors shown in this project were fabricated by P&D, too.

✦ Built specifically for the space — and the way the homeowner uses it.
FEATURED CRAFTSMANSHIP

A pantry that didn't exist before.

What used to be a doorway became a full-height, custom-built pantry designed around the available space. It's the kind of transformation that combines thoughtful planning with hands-on woodworking.

  • Existing doorway converted into cabinetry
  • Custom wood shelving and pull-out storage
  • Deep lower drawers
